Ember Meadows privacy policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Ember Meadows is a game made by Feyerabend Studios ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Ember Meadows on Android.

The short version: Ember Meadows has no accounts, and we don't collect, see or sell your data. Your progress stays on your phone. The free version shows a banner advert provided by Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure those adverts. If you buy "Remove ads", no adverts are shown and AdMob isn't started.

What the game stores on your phone

Ember Meadows keeps a little information in its private storage on your device:

  • which meadows you've reached and which one you're playing
  • your quickest time for each meadow
  • whether sound is on
  • whether you've bought "Remove ads", so the game remembers it before Google Play has answered

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We have no servers that receive it.

Adverts (Google AdMob)

The free version shows one banner advert at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google AdMob. To show adverts, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can suggest your approximate location
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with the advert, such as views and taps

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time it opens, using Google's own consent form. You can choose whether adverts are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Pause › Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google handles this information under Google's privacy policy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services explains more.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-off purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment and holds your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether you own "Remove ads", so it can switch adverts off, including after a reinstall. See Google's privacy policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Permissions

  • Internet access: to load adverts and to reach Google Play for purchases. The game itself plays offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for adverts, as described above.
  • Billing (Google Play): to offer and check the "Remove ads" purchase.

Deleting your data

  • To delete your progress, uninstall Ember Meadows or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
  • To delete or reset your advertising ID, use your phone's settings (on most phones, Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Ember Meadows isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. The game is set up so that only adverts suitable for a general audience are shown.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also say so in the game's release notes.
